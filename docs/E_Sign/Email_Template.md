# How to Use Email Templates?

The **Email Templates** feature in Doculan helps you streamline communication by allowing you to create, save, manage, and reuse email content across your document and form workflows. Instead of rewriting the same message every time, you can define a template once and apply it instantly — ensuring consistent, professional communication with every send.

**Key benefits:**
- Save time by reusing pre-written subject lines and messages.
- Maintain consistent tone and formatting across all outgoing emails.
- Manage a shared library of templates for different document types or audiences.
- Quickly update templates to reflect policy or content changes in one place.

---

## Step 1: Open the Send Mail Window

To begin, navigate to the document or form you wish to send via email.

1. Open the document or form you want to send.
2. Click **Send Mail** to open the email configuration window.

The Send Mail window provides fields for recipient addresses, subject, and message body — along with access to your saved templates.

## Step 2: Create and Save an Email Template

Once the Send Mail window is open, compose your email content and save it as a reusable template.

1. Enter the email **Subject** — use a clear, descriptive line that identifies the purpose of the email.
2. Type your **Message** in the body field. You can include placeholders or dynamic content as needed.
3. Click **Preview** to review how the email will appear to recipients before saving.
4. Click **Save as Template** to open the template naming dialog.
5. In the popup window, enter a **unique name** for the template that makes it easy to identify later.
6. Click **Save** — the template is now added to your Email Template Library and available for future use.

> **Tip:** Use descriptive template names that indicate the document type or audience (e.g., *"NDA Reminder - Client"* or *"Invoice Approval Request"*) to make browsing the library faster.

---

## Step 3: Access the Email Template Library

The Email Template Library is your central repository for all saved templates. You can browse, preview, and select templates from here.

1. Click the **Email Templates** button in the Send Mail window.
2. The **Email Template Library** panel will open, displaying all your saved templates in a list.
3. Browse the list and click on a template to see its subject and message content.
4. Select the template you want to review, use, edit, or delete.

<img src="screenshots\Email Template\Email_Template8.png" style="border:2px solid black; border-radius:4px; width:100%; max-width:800px;" alt="Email Templates button in Send Mail window"><br>

<img src="screenshots\Email Template\Email_Template9.png" style="border:2px solid black; border-radius:4px; width:100%; max-width:800px;" alt="Email Template Library panel with saved templates">

> **Tip:** If you have a large number of templates, use descriptive names with consistent prefixes (e.g., by document type or department) to quickly locate the one you need.

---

## Step 4: Edit an Existing Template

Templates can be updated at any time to reflect new messaging, branding, or process changes.

1. Open the **Email Template Library** and select the template you want to modify.
2. Click **Edit** — the template's subject and message fields become editable.
3. Make your required changes to the subject line, message body, or both.
4. Click **Update** to save the revised version of the template.

The updated template replaces the previous version and will be used in all future applications.

<img src="screenshots\Email Template\Email_Template10.png" style="border:2px solid black; border-radius:4px; width:100%; max-width:800px;" alt="Selecting a template to edit"><br>

<img src="screenshots\Email Template\Email_Template11.png" style="border:2px solid black; border-radius:4px; width:100%; max-width:800px;" alt="Editing the template subject and message">

> **Note:** Editing a template does not affect emails that were already sent using the previous version. Changes only apply to future uses.

---

## Step 5: Use a Template

Apply a saved template to instantly populate the email fields, then customize it if needed before sending.

1. Open the **Email Template Library** and select the template you want to apply.
2. Click **Use Template**.
3. The template's subject and message content will automatically fill in the corresponding fields in the Send Mail window.
4. Review the pre-filled content and make any recipient-specific adjustments as needed.
5. Click **Send** to dispatch the email.

<img src="screenshots\Email Template\Email_Template10.png" style="border:2px solid black; border-radius:4px; width:100%; max-width:800px;" alt="Selecting a template to use"><br>

<img src="screenshots\Email Template\Email_Template13.png" style="border:2px solid black; border-radius:4px; width:100%; max-width:800px;" alt="Template content populated in the email fields">

> **Tip:** Even after applying a template, the subject and message fields remain editable. You can personalize the content before sending without altering the saved template.

---

## Step 6: Delete a Template

Remove templates that are no longer needed to keep your library organized and relevant.

1. Open the **Email Template Library** and select the template you want to remove.
2. Click **Delete**.
3. A confirmation prompt will appear — click **Confirm** to permanently remove the template from the library.

<img src="screenshots\Email Template\Email_Template12.png" style="border:2px solid black; border-radius:4px; width:100%; max-width:800px;" alt="Selecting a template to delete"><br>

<img src="screenshots\Email Template\Email_Template14.png" style="border:2px solid black; border-radius:4px; width:100%; max-width:800px;" alt="Delete confirmation dialog">

> **Warning:** Deleted templates cannot be recovered. If you are unsure, consider editing the template instead of deleting it, or save a copy with a different name before proceeding.

---

## Summary

Email Templates in Doculan eliminate repetitive email drafting and ensure every outgoing communication is consistent and professional. With a centralized template library, you can:

- **Create** templates from any email composition window.
- **Store** them in the Email Template Library for team-wide reuse.
- **Edit** templates at any time to keep messaging up to date.
- **Apply** a template with a single click and personalize it before sending.
- **Delete** outdated templates to keep the library clean and focused.
