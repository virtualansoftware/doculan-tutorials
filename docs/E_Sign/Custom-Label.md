# How to create Custom Labels?

The **Custom Labels** feature allows users to create, organize, and manage labels for documents and contracts. Labels help categorize content, improve document organization, and make it easier to search, filter, and identify documents within the system. Users can create custom labels, assign them to documents or contracts, edit existing labels, and remove labels when they are no longer needed. This feature enhances document management and improves overall workflow efficiency.

---

## Step 1: Open the Manage Labels Window
* Open the E-Sign Editor.
* Click the **Manage Labels** option in the right-side action Window.

## Step 2: Create a New Label
- Click the **Create Label** button.
- Enter a unique and meaningful label name.
- Verify the label details.
- Click Save to create the label.

<img src="screenshots\Labels\Label4.png" style="border:2px solid black; border-radius:4px; width:100%; max-width:800px;" alt="Screenshot for Document">

## Step 3: Assign Labels to Document Fields
- Open the required document or contract.
- Add the required field to the document.
- Double-click the field to open the field configuration panel.
- Navigate to the **Labels** section.
- Select one or more labels from the available list.
- Click Apply or **Save**.

<img src="screenshots\Labels\Label6.png" style="border:2px solid black; border-radius:4px; width:100%; max-width:800px;" alt="Screenshot for Document"><br>

<img src="screenshots\Labels\Label7.png" style="border:2px solid black; border-radius:4px; width:100%; max-width:800px;" alt="Screenshot for Document">

---

## Step 4: Edit an Existing Label
- Open the **Manage Labels** window.
- Locate the label you want to modify.
- Click the **Edit** icon.
- Update the label name or description as required.
- Click **Save** Changes.

<img src="screenshots\Labels\Label8.png" style="border:2px solid black; border-radius:4px; width:100%; max-width:800px;" alt="Screenshot for Document">

---

## Step 5: Delete a Label
- Open the **Manage Labels** window.
- Locate the label you want to remove.
- Click the **Delete** icon.
- Confirm the deletion when prompted.
