﻿# Doculan, eSign Instructions

Welcome to **Doculan**‚ a powerful platform to securely manage and track your digital documents.

---

## Dashboard Overview

The dashboard is your main control center. Here, you can:
- Track the status of all your documents.  
- Create folders to organize and manage your files.  
- Upload a sample contract file for secure storage and easy access.  


<img src=" screenshots\E-Sign_Images\Dashboard.png" style="border:2px solid black; border-radius:4px; width:100%; max-width:800px;" alt="Screenshot for document">

When you click a document name, you'll see detailed tracking info.  
Currently, if no documents are sent for signing, the status will show as empty.

---

### AI Assist

The **AI Assist** chatbot can be opened anytime by selecting **AI Assist** from the toolbar.  
It allows you to ask questions about any document and get quick insights or summaries.
