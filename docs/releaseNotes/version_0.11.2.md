# 🚀 Version 0.11.2 – New Enhancements

## 1️⃣ **Bulk E-Sign**

The **Bulk E-Sign** streamlines large-scale email delivery by automatically sending emails to recipients at scheduled intervals. It provides real-time tracking of delivery status and recipient activity while allowing users to monitor campaigns and resend failed emails from a centralized dashboard.


🔗 **Learn more:** [How to send Bulk mail?](E_Sign/bulk-mail.md)

---

## 2️⃣ **Bulk Form**

The **Bulk Form** feature simplifies large-scale form distribution by allowing you to send a single form to multiple recipients simultaneously. It provides real-time tracking of delivery and submission status, enabling users to monitor responses, manage recipients, and oversee form campaigns from a centralized dashboard.


🔗 **Learn more:** [How to Send Bulk Form in Doculan?](Forms/Form_Bulk.md)

---

## 3️⃣  **Email Templates**

The **Email Templates** feature in Doculan helps you streamline communication by allowing you to create, save, manage, and reuse email content across your document and form workflows. Instead of rewriting the same message every time, you can define a template once and apply it instantly — ensuring consistent, professional communication with every send.

🔗 **Learn more:** [How to Use Email Templates?](E_Sign/Email_Template.md)
