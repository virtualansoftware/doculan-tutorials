# What is “Myself” / “Team” Template?

Templates in **Doculan eSign** are designed to **save time and maintain consistency** across frequently used contracts or agreements.  
They allow you to reuse predefined documents with fields, party roles, and positions already configured — so you don’t have to rebuild them from scratch every time.

Doculan offers **two types of templates**:
1. **Myself Template**
2. **Team Template**

Let’s break them down step-by-step.

---

##  1. Myself Template

The **Myself Template** is for your **personal use only**.  
These templates are visible **only to you** and are best suited for private or one-time workflows.

<details>
  <summary><b>When to Use “Myself” Templates</b></summary>

- You are the **only user** sending or managing the eSign process.  
- You want to maintain **personalized templates** without sharing them with the team.  
- You often send the **same contract format** to different recipients (like NDAs, offer letters, or invoices).  
- You need a **draft-safe workspace** where no one else can modify your templates.
</details>

---

###  Key Features of “Myself” Templates

| Feature | Description |
|----------|--------------|
| Visibility | Private (only visible to the creator) |
| Access | Only you can create, update, or delete |
| Use Case | Individual or recurring personal contracts |
| Sharing | Not shareable with other team members |

---

###  Steps to Create a “Myself” Template

1. Open any contract document you’ve already prepared for eSign.  
2. Configure your desired fields and party structure.  
3. Click the **“Save as Template”** button in the toolbar.  
4. In the popup window, select the **“Myself”** option.  
5. Enter a **template name** (e.g., “Freelance Agreement Template”).  
6. Click **Save Template**.

>  Once saved, this template will be available in your personal template dropdown whenever you start a new eSign document.

---

###  How to Access “Myself” Templates Later

1. Navigate to the **Templates** section in the eSign editor.  
2. Click the dropdown under **Select Template**.  
3. All your **Myself templates** will appear here, sorted alphabetically.  
4. Choose a template and click **Load** — all saved fields and configurations will instantly populate your document.

---

##  2. Team Template

The **Team Template** is designed for **collaboration** and shared workflows.  
These templates are **visible to all members** within your Doculan workspace.

<details>
  <summary><b>When to Use “Team” Templates</b></summary>

- You’re working with a **group of users** sending similar types of contracts.  
- You want everyone on your team to have access to the **same standardized format**.  
- You want consistent branding, layout, and field placements for all documents.  
- You need to reduce repetitive setup steps for each new sender.
</details>

---

###  Key Features of “Team” Templates

| Feature | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Visibility | Shared with all users in your organization |
| Access | Editable by team admins or creators |
| Use Case | Common templates (e.g., client onboarding forms, offer letters) |
| Sharing | Automatically available to your team members |

---

###  Steps to Create a “Team” Template

1. Open an existing document configured with fields, parties, and rules.  
2. Click **Save as Template**.  
3. Select the **“Team”** option.  
4. Enter a **template name** (e.g., “Sales Contract Template”).  
5. Click **Save Template**.  
6. A confirmation message appears:  
   *“Your team template has been saved and shared successfully.”*

>  Team members can now use this template for new eSign requests.

---

###  How to Access “Team” Templates Later

1. Go to **E-Sign → Templates** in the sidebar.  
2. Click the **dropdown** under **Select Template**.  
3. Choose any available **Team Template**.  
4. All predefined fields, positions, and party placeholders will auto-load.  
5. Update any details (like names or emails) and click **Send**.

---

##  Template Components Overview

Both “Myself” and “Team” templates store the following elements:

| Component | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| **Document Structure** | Layout of your uploaded file |
| **Field Mapping** | Signature, Date, Text, and Checkbox placements |
| **Party Roles** | Number of parties and their order |
| **Field Permissions** | Which party can edit which field |
| **E-mail Settings** | Default subject, message, and reminders |
| **Template Type** | “Myself” or “Team” metadata |

---

##  Step-by-Step Example

Let’s say you often send NDAs to clients.

1. You prepare one NDA file with fields for “Name”, “Signature”, and “Date”.  
2. You save it as a **Team Template** named *“Client NDA Template”*.  
3. Your teammates (e.g., HR, Legal) can now use the same template from their dashboards.  
4. When they click **Load Template**, the NDA is pre-filled and ready to send instantly.

>  This improves consistency and reduces setup time by 80%.

##  Permissions & Access Control

| Role | Permissions |
|------|--------------|
| **Template Creator** | Full access — create, edit, delete |
| **Team Member** | Use-only (cannot modify unless given rights) |
| **Admin** | Full access — can edit and delete any team templates |

##  Template Management Tips

-  Use clear, descriptive names like `Client_Agreement_v1` or `Team_Offer_Template`.  
-  Regularly update shared templates to include new compliance clauses.  
-  Notify your team whenever you update a Team Template.  
-  Avoid duplicates by reusing and editing existing templates.  
-  Restrict sensitive templates to “Myself” if privacy is needed.
