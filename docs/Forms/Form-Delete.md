# How to Delete Form?

This guide explains how to **delete a form in Doculan**. A form can only be deleted if it has **no linked recipients or submissions**.<br>
To delete a form, navigate to the **Documents** section, select the **appropriate folder**, open the **form** Actions menu, and select **Delete** to permanently remove it. A confirmation pop-up will appear, allowing you to review and verify the action before final deletion.<br>
This process ensures that only unused or draft forms are safely removed from the system, maintaining data integrity and compliance.

---

##  **Step:1 Navigate to Delete the Form**

- Forms can be deleted **only if no recipients are linked**
- Navigate to the **Documents and select the appropriate folder**.
- Locate the **form** and click the **⋮ (three-dot) icon** under the Actions column.
- Click **Delete.**
