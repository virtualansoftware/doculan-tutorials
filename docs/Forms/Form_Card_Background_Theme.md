# How to Customize the Form Theme?

The **Theme** feature allows you to customize the appearance of a form by configuring the **Form Card** and **Background**. You can apply colors or images to create a consistent and visually appealing form design.

   * **Form Card**
   * **Background**

## Step 1: Customize the Form Card
The **Form Card** settings allow you to customize the appearance of the main form card.

**Apply a Solid Color**

1. Select **Form Card**.
2. Choose **Solid Color**.
3. Select the required color or enter a custom color.
4. Adjust the **Opacity** using the slider.
5. Review the changes in the form preview.

<img src="screenshots\Form-Card-Background-Theme\Form-Solid-Color.png" alt="Step 1 — Clone the Form" style="border:2px solid black; border-radius:4px; width:100%; max-width:800px;"><br>

 **Add a Form Card Image**

1. Select **Form Card**.
2. Choose **Image**.
3. Click **Click to upload image**.
4. Upload the required image.
5. Adjust the **Opacity** as required.
6. Review the updated form card.


<img src="screenshots\Form-Card-Background-Theme\Form-Card-Add-Image.png" alt="Step 1 — Clone the Form" style="border:2px solid black; border-radius:4px; width:100%; max-width:800px;">

## Step 2: Customize the Background

The **Background** settings allow you to customize the area surrounding the form card.

**Apply a Solid Color**

1. Select **Background**.
2. Choose **Solid Color**.
3. Select the required color or enter a custom color.
4. Adjust the **Opacity** using the slider.
5. Review the changes in the form preview.

<img src="screenshots\Form-Card-Background-Theme\Form-Backround.png" alt="Step 1 — Clone the Form" style="border:2px solid black; border-radius:4px; width:100%; max-width:800px;"><br>

 **Add a Background Image**

1. Select **Background**.
2. Choose **Image**.
3. Click **Click to upload image**.
4. Upload the required background image.
5. Alternatively, select an image from the available **Default Images**.
6. Adjust the **Opacity** as required.
7. Review the form preview.


<img src="screenshots\Form-Card-Background-Theme\Form-Backround-Add.png" alt="Step 1 — Clone the Form" style="border:2px solid black; border-radius:4px; width:100%; max-width:800px;">

> **Note:** You can customize the **Form Card** and **Background** independently using either a solid color or image to create the desired form appearance.

---

### Step 3: Form Preview

The **Form Preview** feature allows users to review the form’s appearance and layout before publishing or sharing it. It displays the configured **Form Card**, **Background**, colors, images, form title, description, and fields as they will appear to users.

 **Preview the Form**

1. Click **Form Preview** after applying the desired theme settings.
2. Review the **Form Card** and **Background** appearance.
3. Verify that the uploaded images, colors, title, description, and form fields are displayed correctly.
4. Scroll through the preview to review the complete form layout.
5. Close the preview and make any required changes to the theme or form design.

<img src="screenshots\Form-Card-Background-Theme\Form-Preview.png" alt="Step 1 — Clone the Form" style="border:2px solid black; border-radius:4px; width:100%; max-width:800px;">

> **Note:** Use **Form Preview** each time you update the theme to verify the appearance before finalizing the form.

---

##  Step 4: Form Theme Applied

The customized theme is displayed on the live form after the **Form Card** and **Background** settings are applied. Users can view the selected colors, images, form layout, and fields together in the final form.

**Review the Customized Form**

1. The selected **Background** color or image is applied to the overall form page.
2. The customized **Form Card** appearance is applied to the main form section.
3. The form title, fields, buttons, and other elements are displayed with the selected theme.
4. Review the complete form to ensure the colors and images are displayed as expected.
5. Make any required theme adjustments and preview the form again before sharing or publishing it.

<img src="screenshots\Form-Card-Background-Theme\Form-Theme-Applied.png" alt="Step 1 — Clone the Form" style="border:2px solid black; border-radius:4px; width:100%; max-width:800px;">

> **Note:** Use the **Form Preview** option to verify the final appearance whenever the theme is updated.
