# How to Resend a Shared Document?

Sometimes recipients may miss your shared email, or the access link might expire before they view the document.  
Doculan makes it easy to **resend shared documents** instantly — without re-uploading or reconfiguring sharing settings.

---

##  Overview

Resending allows you to:
- Reissue expired or unopened links.
- Update recipient details (e.g., corrected email).
- Maintain a complete tracking history for compliance.

>  *You can only resend documents with a status of **Expired** or **Unopened**. Completed or cancelled shares cannot be resent.*

---

##  Step-by-Step: Resending a Shared Document

<details>
  <summary><b>Step 1: Go to your dashboard</b></summary>

- Navigate to your **Dashboard** after logging into Doculan.  
- Open the folder that contains your shared document (for example, “e-sign documents” or “Shared Contracts”).  
- You’ll see a table with columns — *Name*, *Party Details*, *Last Modified*, *Created By*, *Status*, and *Actions*.

> 🖼️ **Screenshot Placeholder:** Dashboard showing document table with status column.
</details>

---

<details>
  <summary><b>Step 2: Identify the document to resend</b></summary>

- In the **Status** column, look for the document marked as **Expired** or **Unopened**.  
- These are eligible for resending.  
- You can also verify the activity by clicking the **Audit** button — it displays timestamps and recipient details.

> 🖼️ **Screenshot Placeholder:** Document list showing expired/unopened status and audit button.
</details>

---

<details>
  <summary><b>Step 3: Open the Actions menu</b></summary>

- In the same row as your document, click the **three-dot (⋮)** icon under the **Actions** column.  
- You’ll see several options — *View*, *E-Sign*, *Share*, *Download*, *Delete*.  
- Select **Share** again to open the sharing window.

> 🖼️ **Screenshot Placeholder:** Actions dropdown showing Share option highlighted.
</details>

---

<details>
  <summary><b>Step 4: Verify and edit recipient details</b></summary>

In the share window:
- Review recipient’s name and email address.  
- Update the email if it was entered incorrectly.  
- Set a **new expiry date** and **reminder** if required.  
- (Optional) Add a note such as “Resending the document for your reference.”

> 🖼️ **Screenshot Placeholder:** Share popup with updated email and expiry settings.
</details>

---

<details>
  <summary><b>Step 5: Send the document again</b></summary>

- Once you’ve verified all details, click the **Send** button.  
- The system will resend a new secure link to the recipient’s inbox.  
- You’ll receive a success notification confirming the document was resent.

> 🖼️ **Screenshot Placeholder:** “Resend successful” confirmation message.
</details>

---

<details>
  <summary><b>Step 6: Track resend status</b></summary>

After resending:
- Go back to your **Dashboard**.  
- Refresh the folder view — the document’s **Status** will change to **Resent** or **Shared (Active)**.  
- You can click **Audit** again to view the new send timestamp and updated email trail.

> 🖼️ **Screenshot Placeholder:** Audit trail showing resend timestamp.
</details>

---

##  Example Scenario

Let’s say you previously shared “Employee_Contract.pdf” with a new hire, but the link expired before they opened it.

1. Open the e-sign folder.  
2. Locate the file with **Status: Expired**.  
3. Click **⋮ → Share**.  
4. Update expiry to **3 days**, verify recipient email.  
5. Click **Send**.  
6. Doculan generates a new secure link and updates your audit trail automatically.

>  The recipient receives a new email with a fresh access link.

---

##  Important Notes

| Condition | Action | Notes |
|------------|---------|-------|
| Expired | Resend available | Must set new expiry date |
| Unopened | Resend available | Use same or updated email |
| Completed | Not allowed | Document already viewed/signed |
| Cancelled | Not allowed | Document link is deactivated |
| Audit Trail | Always updated | Shows all resend activity |

---

##  Best Practices

-  Always double-check email addresses before resending.  
-  Use **Resend** only for expired/unopened links — not for edited files.  
-  Keep expiry dates short for security.  
-  Avoid resending sensitive files multiple times unnecessarily.  
-  Regularly review the Audit Trail for compliance tracking.
