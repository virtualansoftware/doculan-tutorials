# How to Delete a Template?

Sometimes you no longer need a particular eSign template — maybe the contract version has expired, or your organization has moved to a new format.  
In such cases, you can **safely delete** templates from Doculan to keep your workspace organized and clutter-free.

---

##  Overview

Template deletion in **Doculan eSign** allows you to permanently remove unused or outdated templates from your account or shared team list.

You can delete:
- **Personal (Myself)** templates → visible only to you.  
- **Team** templates → shared among your organization/team members.

>  **Note:** Deleted templates cannot be recovered. Make sure to double-check before confirming deletion.

---

##  Step-by-Step: Deleting an Existing Template

<details>
  <summary><b>Step 1: Access the eSign section</b></summary>

- From your **Dashboard**, open the folder where you manage your eSign documents (e.g., “e-sign documents”).  
- Click on the **three-dot (⋮)** menu under **Actions** for any document.  
- Choose **E-Sign** from the dropdown to open the document in the eSign editor.

> 🖼️ **Screenshot Placeholder:** Dashboard showing ⋮ → E-Sign selection.
</details>

---

<details>
  <summary><b>Step 2: Open the Template tab</b></summary>

- Once the eSign editor loads, click on the **Template** tab (usually found on the right-hand panel).  
- You’ll see a **dropdown menu** showing all available templates under two sections:
  - Myself (personal templates)
  - Team (shared templates)

> 🖼️ **Screenshot Placeholder:** Template tab showing dropdown of templates.
</details>

---

<details>
  <summary><b>Step 3: Select the template you want to delete</b></summary>

- In the Template dropdown, select the name of the template you want to remove.  
- Ensure it’s the correct one — check the **Last Modified** date or review its contents to confirm.

> 🖼️ **Screenshot Placeholder:** Template selection dropdown with highlighted template name.
</details>

---

<details>
  <summary><b>Step 4: Click the “Delete Template” button</b></summary>

- After selecting the template, click the **Delete Template** button below the dropdown.  
- A confirmation prompt will appear asking:
  > “Are you sure you want to delete this template permanently?”

- Click **Yes / Confirm** to proceed.

> 🖼️ **Screenshot Placeholder:** Confirmation popup with “Yes / No” buttons.
</details>

---

<details>
  <summary><b>Step 5: Verify deletion</b></summary>

- Once deleted, the template will be removed from the dropdown list immediately.  
- You can refresh the page or reopen the Template tab to ensure it’s no longer listed.  
- If it’s a **Team Template**, other team members will also lose access to it.

> 🖼️ **Screenshot Placeholder:** Template dropdown with deleted template removed.
</details>

---

##  Example Scenario

Let’s say you have a team template named **"Service_Contract_2023"**, but now you’ve created a newer version called **"Service_Contract_2025"**.

To clean up:
1. Go to the eSign editor → Template tab.  
2. Select `Service_Contract_2023`.  
3. Click **Delete Template**.  
4. Confirm the deletion.  
5. Now, only your latest version appears for use.

---

##  Important Notes

| Action | Impact | Recovery |
|---------|---------|-----------|
| Delete from Myself | Removes only from your account |  Not recoverable |
| Delete from Team | Removes for all team members |  Not recoverable |
| Delete during editing | Removes instantly, even if document open |  Refresh list manually |
| Delete while used in active workflow | Template removed, but existing documents remain safe |  Safe |

##  Best Practices

-  Always confirm with your team before deleting a **shared (Team)** template.  
-  Consider exporting or copying template details before deletion (for backup).  
-  Use version naming (like `_v1`, `_v2`) to distinguish old templates before removal.  
-  Restrict deletion access to authorized admins for shared environments.  
-  Schedule quarterly cleanups for unused templates to keep things tidy.

---

##  Summary

By deleting unnecessary templates, you keep your workspace clean, reduce confusion, and ensure only the latest versions are in use.  
Once deleted, templates are permanently removed — so always double-check before confirming.
